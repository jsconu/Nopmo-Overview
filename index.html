Program intelligence for messy work

Nopmo organizes fragmented work for you — then finds what actually needs attention.

Work rarely starts as a clean project model. Nopmo begins with the systems where it already lives — project tools, email, chat, meetings, calendars, and documents — and proposes the initiatives, relationships, conflicting commitments, and gaps it thinks are there.

People confirm or correct the structure instead of rebuilding it by hand. Nopmo is not trying to replace Jira, Smartsheet, Monday, or another system of record; it is the management-intelligence layer above the messy reality those systems collectively represent.

Start with evidence, not setup

Connect sources first. Nopmo groups related signals, preserves alternate names, proposes likely project boundaries, and surfaces ambiguity when it cannot tell whether two labels describe the same work.

Discover work

Find substantial initiatives already underway, even when nobody created them in Nopmo.

Resolve identity

Recognize that “Customer Portal,” “Portal Phase 2,” and a recurring meeting may describe the same initiative — without silently merging them.

Ask only where judgment matters

Humans confirm, correct, decide, and authorize. Nopmo should avoid asking them to retype facts available in source evidence.

What Nopmo is built to notice

Consequences across projects

A delivery problem in one initiative can expose testing, launch, training, or customer commitments elsewhere. Nopmo models those relationships explicitly.

Disagreement between sources

If Jira, email, and a meeting contain incompatible dates or commitments, Nopmo surfaces the conflict instead of quietly choosing one and pretending certainty.

Whether a decision worked

A recommendation is not success. Nopmo can retain the expected outcome of a decision and later compare it with evidence from execution.

From fragmented signals to management attention

The underlying system preserves evidence and project state, but the user experience is intentionally simpler: show the small number of things that deserve attention, explain why they matter, and make the next decision or action clear.

1. Observe

Read the systems where execution already happens

Source identity, timestamps, freshness, and provenance are retained.

2. Connect

Reason across project boundaries

Relationships, shared resources, commitments, risks, and conflicting claims change the meaning of otherwise local signals.

3. Intervene

Surface a decision, bounded action, or outcome check

Consequential actions stay behind deterministic policy and human review. Model confidence never grants authority.
